\section{Recursive Targets and the Pure-Self-Loop Guard}
\label{sec:recursive-boundary}

This appendix resolves the registered scope limitation of the structural
junk rejection (Section~\ref{sec:threshold-gate}): forbidding the mining
target from length-2 rule bodies removes the self-referential junk rule
without gold labels, but forecloses genuinely recursive targets
(\emph{ancestor}~$\leftarrow$~\emph{ancestor.parent}), so the flag stays
opt-in and licenses ``safe on non-recursive targets'' only. Three locked,
deterministic, fully replayable results at zero provider cost replace that
limitation with a strictly better mechanism: a guard that bans \emph{only}
the pure all-target body (both inversions of
\emph{target}~$\times$~\emph{target}) while keeping mixed
\emph{target}~$\times$~\emph{base} legs.

\paragraph{Result 1: the blunt ban measurably forecloses recursion (synthetic).}
On a seeded random DAG ($n{=}150$, edge probability $0.03$) whose target is
the genuine transitive closure over a base relation, with oracle teachers
and a $3$-seed $\times$ $3$-threshold ($\gamma \in \{0.50, 0.70, 0.90\}$)
$\times$ $3$-arm grid (published / blunt ban / refined guard), the blunt
arm loses forward-chaining coverage of the full closure on \textbf{9/9
recursive cells} (e.g.\ $0.7065 \rightarrow 0.5038$), while the refined arm
preserves published-arm coverage on \textbf{9/9} cells and drops exactly
the pure self-loop installs, nothing else. Unsound derivations are $0$ in
every cell: on a genuinely transitive target even the pure self-loop is
sound --- a subset of the closure --- hence redundant rather than harmful,
which is why coverage preservation and junk removal can coexist. Artifact:
\texttt{experiments/results/e18\_recursive\_target\_synthetic.json}.

\paragraph{Result 2: the refined guard subsumes the published junk rejection on the real grid (classification).}
Re-classifying every \texttt{synthesised\_rules} entry of the committed
MetaQA 2-hop artifact ($22$ cells, both arms), the control arm's
target-containing rules classify \textbf{14/14} as pure self-loops, spread
over exactly the $7$ published junk cells; the true compositions are kept
in \textbf{10/10} cells, every composition rule base-only; and the totals
reconcile with the published aggregates exactly, per cell as well as in
aggregate. Locked decision: \textbf{REFINED-GUARD-SUBSUMES} --- the
refined guard keeps the published junk-rejection behaviour on the
non-recursive grid exactly; combined with Result 1, it is
coverage-preserving on genuinely recursive targets, while the blunt
\texttt{--forbid-target-in-body} flag stays opt-in and non-recursive-only.
Artifact: \texttt{experiments/results/e16\_metaqa\_reread\_refined\_guard.json},
contract-tested in \texttt{tests/test\_e16\_replay\_refined.py}.

\paragraph{Result 3: generative re-mining on the real grid.}
Re-mining all $22$ recorded cells at $\gamma{=}0.50$ under the refined
guard --- teacher answers replayed from the archived recordings, zero
provider calls --- the control arm reproduces the published shape exactly
under the refined taxonomy ($14$ pure-self-loop rules over exactly the $7$
published junk cells; true installs $10/10$), and the refined arm shows
\textbf{zero} pure self-loop and \textbf{zero} mixed-recursive rules across
all $22$ cells, with true compositions kept in \textbf{10/10} at identical
per-rule world precision, no valid cell losing accuracy, and $0$ invalid
cells. Locked decision: \textbf{GENERATIVE-SUBSUMES} --- the junk removal
is not a classification artifact of committed rule lists; it holds when
the miner runs fresh under the guard. Artifact:
\texttt{experiments/results/e18\_w2\_generative\_refined.json}; runner
plumbing and contract test shipped in the same commit.

\paragraph{Explicit non-claims.}
(1) Near-functional leakage (Section~\ref{sec:rule_precision}) is
untouched; the admission-mechanism question remains closed-inconclusive
and is not cited as support. (2) Scope is miner-level: a synthetic
transitive-closure grid with oracle teachers, a classification replay, and
a generative replay of the MetaQA 2-hop grid --- no deployment, no
product, no benchmark beyond MetaQA 2-hop. (3) The archived teacher
recordings stay in the authoring archive; the public artifacts carry
per-cell outcomes only, never raw paid answers.

\paragraph{Reproducibility.}
All three results are deterministic and replayable at zero provider cost
from the seeds and the committed artifacts:
\texttt{experiments/run\_e18\_recursive.py},
\texttt{experiments/replay\_e16\_refined.py}, and
\texttt{experiments/run\_real\_kg\_controlled.py} (replay mode), with
contract tests \texttt{tests/test\_e18\_recursive.py},
\texttt{tests/test\_e16\_replay\_refined.py}, and
\texttt{tests/test\_controlled\_selfloop\_plumbing.py}.
