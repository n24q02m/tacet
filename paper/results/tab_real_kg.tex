\noindent\emph{Oracle 2-hop mechanism isolation (same stream, 78 distinct
heads).} With an oracle Tier-3 teacher the miner recovers the genuine
composition rule
$q2(x,y)\!\leftarrow\!\mathit{starred\_actors}^{-1}(x,z)\wedge\mathit{directed\_by}(z,y)$,
so full distillation answers 2-hop questions for \emph{unseen} heads at
Tier~1: \textbf{10} teacher calls vs the cache's \textbf{78}
(\textbf{87\% fewer}), both at accuracy $1.0$. Under the real Grok teacher
($\sim$22--54\% accurate on the 2-hop stream across seeds) the miner instead
recovers \emph{no} installable rule, so distillation matches the cache exactly.
This is the distillation-over-caching effect, isolated: it materialises only
when the teacher is accurate enough for the miner to recover a generalising rule.
